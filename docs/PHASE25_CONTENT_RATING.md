# Phase 25 content rating and target audience

Canonical working answers:
`release/play-store/content-rating.json`

Recommended internal-beta target audience: **Adults 18+**.

PulseChat contains user interaction, private direct/group text, image sharing
and user-generated content. Blocking, reporting and privacy controls exist, but
moderation is reactive and no automated/pre-publication content filtering is
claimed. The app has no advertising, gambling, purchases, location sharing or
app-supplied mature editorial content.

When completing IARC/Play questions:

1. Answer yes to user interaction, text sharing, photo sharing and UGC where
   asked.
2. Answer yes to available block/report controls.
3. Answer no to proactive content filtering or verified moderation guarantees.
4. Answer no to app-supplied violence, sexual content, profanity, gambling,
   controlled substances and advertising unless the shipped app changes.
5. Do not target children or select a Families category for Prototype V1.
6. Save a PDF/screenshot of the submitted answers and resulting rating in the
   Phase 25 evidence folder.

The current Play questionnaire remains authoritative; this source record is a
truthful working basis, not a prediction of the final rating label.
