PulseChat Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-29

This policy explains how Prakash Dash (“we”) handles information for the PulseChat Android app and its supporting services.

Information we handle

  • Account information such as email address, authentication identifiers and session records.
  • Profile information such as display name, optional username, bio and avatar.
  • Direct and group messages, photos/captions, replies, edits, deletions, reactions, delivery/read states and timestamps.
  • Conversation membership, notification/mute choices, privacy settings, blocks and private safety reports.
  • Presence/last-seen status and ephemeral typing events, subject to privacy controls.
  • Search queries processed to return authorized people, conversation and message results.
  • Expo push token, platform, optional device name, app version and push-delivery status when notifications are enabled.

PulseChat does not intentionally collect location, contacts, financial or health data, audio, advertising data, or files outside supported images.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users; operate profiles and conversations; synchronize and search authorized messages; provide receipts, presence, notifications and offline recovery; enforce security, privacy, blocks and rate limits; process reports; and delete accounts. PulseChat has no ads or in-app purchases and does not sell personal data.

Service providers

PulseChat uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage, realtime and server functions; Expo Push Service for push routing; and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android notification transport. These providers process information on our behalf under their terms and configured retention.

Security and message access

Information is encrypted in transit. PulseChat uses authenticated access, database authorization policies, private storage controls and encrypted native local state. PulseChat V1 is not end-to-end encrypted. Authorized backend services can process message content to provide storage, synchronization, search, reports and notification previews when enabled.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in PulseChat from Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account, or use our public account-deletion page. Account deletion removes the authentication account and associated profile, membership, settings, privacy, block, report and push-token records.

Shared messages and photos may remain as anonymized conversation history after account deletion so other participants retain their conversation record. Delete-for-everyone immediately redacts message and attachment metadata; physical image cleanup is best effort. Limited security and operational logs may remain according to provider configuration or legal requirements.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, choose notification/privacy settings, mute chats, block users, report interactions, delete messages where available, sign out and delete your account. Android permissions for notifications, camera and photos can be denied or changed in system settings.

Age

The controlled PulseChat beta is intended for adults aged 18 or older and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app, processors or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date and publish the revised policy here.
