Delete your PulseChat account

Developer: Prakash Dash

Delete inside the app

  1. Sign in to PulseChat.
  2. Open Profile → Settings → Account.
  3. Select Delete account, review the consequences and confirm.

Request deletion without the app

Email praksdash81@gmail.com from the email address used for your PulseChat account. Use the subject “PulseChat account deletion request.” Do not send a password, authentication token or message content. We may ask you to complete a safe ownership-verification step before deletion.

What is deleted

The authentication account and linked profile, conversation membership, settings, privacy choices, blocks, reports and push-token registrations are removed. Profile and empty-group avatar cleanup is attempted.

What may remain

Messages and photos shared with other conversation members may remain as anonymized conversation history. Message delete-for-everyone redacts durable message and attachment metadata immediately; physical image cleanup is best effort. Limited security, deletion-request and operational records may be retained only as needed for completion, abuse prevention, provider retention or legal obligations.

For details, read the PulseChat Privacy Policy.
